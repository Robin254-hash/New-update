## Project quality checklist
- [ ] State the objective and authorized scope.
- [ ] Capture genuine screenshots and remove secrets or sensitive information.
- [ ] Include the commands or process used and explain the results.
- [ ] Separate observed facts from assumptions.
- [ ] Record limitations and practical remediation.
